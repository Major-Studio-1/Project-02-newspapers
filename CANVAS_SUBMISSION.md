Project 02 — Newspapers, Attention, and Connected Events

Repository: https://github.com/Major-Studio-1/Project-02-newspapers

The README presents three concepts using the Revolution Crossroads historical newspaper dataset: The Afterlife of Attention, News in Transit, and The Event Telescope. Each includes a short concept description, the data to explore, quantitative and qualitative dimensions, traditional chart options, and an expressive visualization proposal. Three AI-assisted digital concept sketches are included in the sketches directory, with their production method disclosed. They are not scans of student hand drawings; please advise whether this form is acceptable for the sketch requirement.
